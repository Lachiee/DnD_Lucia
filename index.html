The Lazy Dungeon Master's Tome

Eight Steps to a Memorable Game · After Sly Flourish's Return of the Lazy DM

Step 1 Review the Characters

Before anything else — who are the characters? What do they want? What hook can you include this session for each one?

Manual Character Hooks This Session

For each character, jot the hook you'll use to draw them into this session.

Step 2 Create a Strong Start

"Something happens." Begin in the middle of the action — draw players out of the real world and into the game immediately.

Step 3 Outline Potential Scenes

What scenes might occur? Keep these brief — one scene per ~45 min of play. Don't over-plan; be ready to discard these entirely.

Encounters & Situations

Possible combat, social, or exploration beats. Leave room to improvise based on pacing.

Step 4 Define Secrets & Clues

The heart of the eight steps. Write up to ten secrets — short, relevant, and not tied to a specific location or method of discovery. You decide how they're revealed during play.

Step 5 Develop Fantastic Locations

What locations might come up? A name and a few evocative features are often enough. Add 1–3 "aspects" for set-piece locations.

Step 6 Outline Important NPCs

Name, one distinguishing trait, and what they want. Consider grabbing mannerisms from a character in fiction for instant depth.

Step 7 Choose Relevant Monsters

List potential monsters separately from encounters so you can drop them in anywhere based on pacing and situation. Include the lazy encounter benchmark.

Step 8 Select Treasure & Magic Items

Choose items that fit specific characters or roll randomly — mixing both works well. Players love loot; it's worth a few minutes of prep.
