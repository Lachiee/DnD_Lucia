Step 2Create a Strong Start

"Something happens." Begin in the middle of the action. What's the first thing the players experience?

Step 3Outline Potential Scenes

~1 scene per 45 min. Keep these brief — be ready to throw them away. Include adventure hooks, forks, and options.

Step 4Define Secrets & Clues

The heart of the eight steps. Short, relevant, not tied to a location or discovery method. Up to ten — carry forward undiscovered ones from last session.

Step 5Develop Fantastic Locations

A name and 1–3 evocative aspects is often enough. For set-piece battles add interactive features.

Step 6Outline Important NPCs

Name, one distinguishing trait, what they want. Base on a fiction character for instant mannerisms.

Step 7Choose Relevant Monsters

List separately from encounters so you can drop them anywhere based on pacing. Note the lazy encounter benchmark.

Step 8Select Treasure & Magic Items

Choose items that fit specific characters OR roll randomly — mix both. Players love loot.
